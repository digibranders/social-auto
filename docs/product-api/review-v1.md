# Review service v1: what a product builds its screens on

Status: draft for the owner's review, built locally and not deployed. The shapes below are held by `scheduler/engine/src/review-v1-contract.test.ts`; real responses are in `review-v1-examples.json` (regenerate with `REVIEW_V1_EXAMPLES=<file> node --test src/review-v1-contract.test.ts`).

## Who is calling

Every request from a product sends:

| Header | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `Authorization` | `Bearer <the product's own secret>` (`REVIEW_PRODUCT_SECRETS="brandbasket:<secret>"` on the service) |
| `X-Brand` | the brand this request is for, chosen by the product (`bb-<workspaceId>`), never taken from the website |
| `X-Actor` | who is deciding, `<productId>:<endUserId>`; recorded as the approver, and any `by` in a body is ignored |

A product sees only its `X-Brand`. Any other brand answers `404`. Staff keep read-only access to product brands; every change from the staff secret answers `403 {code:"FORBIDDEN"}`.

## Errors

`{ "error": "<plain sentence>", "code": "<CODE>" }` with: `400 INVALID`, `403 FORBIDDEN`, `404 NOT_FOUND`, `409 STALE | ALREADY_DECIDED | NOT_APPROVED | BLOCKED | CONFLICT | LIMIT | ALREADY_DRAWN | CLOSED`, `422 REJECTED`, `503 LOCKED` (busy, try again). Messages are written for the owner today; a customer-safe wording layer is piece 6.

## Calls

| Call | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `POST /review/jobs` `{kind:"copy", site, brand, networks, weeks?, postsPerWeek?, days?, startDate?, time?, page?}` | read the website and write posts for the chosen networks. The first call claims `brand` for the product (409 `CONFLICT` if the name is taken). Answers `202 {job, created}`. |
| `GET /review/jobs`, `GET /review/jobs/<id>` | progress (`state` queued/running/succeeded/failed, `step` 1 to 4, `error:{code,message}`) |
| `GET /review/brands/<brand>/facts` | the facts found on the site: `{brand, builtAt, mode, profile, facts[{factId,text,sourceUrl,sourceQuote,state,held,heldReason?}], counts}` |
| `POST /review/brands/<brand>/facts/<factId>/confirm\|remove\|restore` | answers the updated facts view; a held fact cannot be confirmed |
| `GET /review/copy` | the written posts waiting for a decision: `{batches[{brand, planFile, createdAt, networks[], posts[{postId, headline, caption, captions{<network>:{text, edited?, fallback?}}, state, contentHash, edited?, advice?}], signoffDefault?}], networkRules}` |
| `POST /review/copy/<brand>/<planFile>/draw` `{week?, retry?}` | draw the kept posts. A batch whose posts have days (`posts[].date`) is drawn one week at a time: `week` is the Monday (`400 INVALID` for a week the batch does not have), else the first week not drawn or given up. The list (`GET /review/copy`) then has `weeks:[{from,to,state:waiting\|drawing\|drawn\|lapsed,postIds}]`. A week asked twice is the same job (`job.week`); once a week is asked for, the keep, drop and edit of its posts answer `409 ALREADY_DRAWN` while later weeks stay open. A kept topic that got no picture in its week's draw is recorded (`posts[].notDrawn`, `weeks[].notDrawn`: `{code: HEADLINE_TOO_WEAK|NO_PICTURE|PICTURE_LIMIT_REACHED, reason}`) and stays open: keep, drop and edit work again for it while no draw of its week is waiting or running, and `draw {week, retry:true}` (after the draw failed) draws only such topics. The weekly runner never retries it. A batch counts as drawn when every week is drawn or lapsed. **Automatic weekly draw:** the service itself asks for the draw of every week after the first for a product's brand, on the Thursday before the week begins at 10:00 India time, when the customer kept topics in it and the week has room for the pictures (job `by: system:weekly-draw`, `auto: true`, never counted as the customer's own ask); a week that ended undrawn becomes `lapsed`. `AUTO_WEEK_DRAW=false` turns it off; it looks every 10 minutes. `PUT /review/connected {networks}` (product only) records which networks the customer has connected accounts for; a draw buys no picture for any other (never recorded: every network the posts were written for). A batch written before the days were recorded is drawn in one piece, as before. |
| `POST /review/copy/<brand>/<planFile>/<postId>/keep\|drop` | the headline and words approval, per post |
| `POST /review/copy/<brand>/<planFile>/<postId>/check` `{headline?, caption?, network?}` | runs the checks of an edit (length, writing, and the claim gate against the post's facts) on words that are not saved, and changes nothing. Works after a draw. `200 {ok:true}` or `422 {code:"REJECTED"}` with the reason. |
| `PUT /review/copy/<brand>/<planFile>/<postId>/edit` `{headline, caption, network?, brandName?, domain?}` | change words; checked against the site's facts |
| `POST /review/copy/<brand>/<planFile>/networks` `{network}` | add a network to a written batch (waiting or already drawn), from the same topics and with no new website read. A **job** (`kind:"network"`, answer `202 {job, created}`; a second click while it runs returns `200` with the same job), because the captions are written with the AI key that only the job runner holds. For each topic (the kept ones once drawn, the ones not dropped while waiting) it writes the network's caption with the writer's own claim gate and the network's length limit, and stores it in the plan and the copy list; a caption that could not pass is replaced by the main caption adapted to the network and marked `fallback:true` with a fixed `reasonCode` (`WORDS_REFUSED`, `TOO_LONG`, `NO_FACTS` or `NO_CAPTION`, never free text); it is never skipped, so `outcome` is always `written`, and the customer can change the words. The batch's release record gains the network. For a drawn topic: every network gets a picture of its own (square for instagram, facebook and google; wide for linkedin), so a topic that already has pictures gets `drawJobId` of a `kind:"newnet"` job that draws one picture for that network with a composition of its own (it does not use the redraw allowance); another network's picture is never copied. Each picture's headline lettering is art-directed, not picked from a menu: the picture model is asked to invent an original typographic voice from the headline, the topic and the brand (a vocabulary of weight, character, contrast, width, rhythm and treatment is offered as inspiration only), and chooses placement, scale, line breaks and alignment itself. A firm lean (a weight, a character, an attitude and a way of using the brand's own colours on the lettering) that is different for each network of a topic, follows from the post (so a redraw keeps it) and steers clear of the brand's last 20 leans is given as a strong nudge the model may set aside only when the words truly call for it; script is allowed only as one short accent word. The spelling and legibility checks are unchanged. `HEADLINE_STYLES_ENABLED=false` in the engine's settings switches it off. Nothing already approved changes; the new versions need approving. `GET /review/jobs/<id>` carries `result:{network, topics:[{postId, outcome:"written", fallback?, reasonCode?, versionIds?, drawJobId?}]}` once it succeeds. Errors: `400 INVALID` (unknown network), `409 CONFLICT` (the batch already has it), `409 CLOSED` (put away or expired), `404`, `403` for staff on a product brand. About $0.002 per topic for text, plus about $0.017 for each picture drawn. The job runner queues a picture only for as many topics as the channel has room for this week (soonest first; a client of ours has no limit); the review service's weekly pass then draws the rest as room comes (`newnet` jobs, `by: system:weekly-draw`, `auto: true`): for each kept topic of a drawn week with a picture for some network but none for a connected network it was written for, up to the room left, never from a past day, never again after a failure that was not the limit. A product's customer may draw 7 pictures a week for each channel (Monday to Sunday, India time; `PRODUCT_PICTURES_PER_CHANNEL_WEEK`, 7 by default, never above 20); the service reads the engine's paid-call ledger (`PAID_LEDGER_FILE`, the same file as the engine's) and refuses a draw, redraw or added network that would pass a channel's limit with `429 {error, code:"PICTURE_LIMIT_REACHED", channel, restart}` before a job is queued. `GET /review/limits` (answers `503 LOCKED` when the paid-call ledger cannot be found while the brand already has pictures, instead of showing an untouched count: the engine and this service must read the same file, `PAID_LEDGER_FILE` set in both or in neither; the service logs the path it reads at start-up) answers `{brand, week:{from,to,restart}, limit, channels:[{network, limit, used, left}]}` for the product's brand (staff: `?brand=`; `404` for a brand no product owns). Our own clients keep the dollar caps only. A backstop of at most 200 pictures a brand a month stays in the engine. |
| `POST /review/jobs` `{kind:"carousel", site, brand, networks, startDate?, time?}` | a product's customer asks for this week's carousel (`brand` must be the one in `X-Brand`). Only the networks she has connected are made for (`409 CONFLICT` "CONNECTED_UNKNOWN" until `PUT /review/connected` was called, `400 INVALID` when none of those asked for is connected). One a week that is still wanted, and at most two made in a week (Monday to Sunday, India time, from the day it is for): beyond that `429 {error, code:"CAROUSEL_LIMIT_REACHED", restart}`. Answers `202 {job, created}` (`kind:"carousel"`; a second ask while one runs is the same job). Staff may ask for any brand named after its site, as before. In placeholder mode the slides are plain stand-ins and cost nothing. |
| `GET /review/carousel-weekly`, `PUT /review/carousel-weekly` `{enabled}` | whether the weekly carousel is on for the product's brand (off by default); only a product may use it (`404` for staff). The review service's weekly pass (`AUTO_WEEK_DRAW`, every 10 minutes) makes one carousel job (`kind: carousel`, `auto: true`, `by: system:weekly-carousel`) from the Thursday before the week at 10:00 India time for its Friday, when the connected channels are known and non-empty, the website was read, a written batch exists, the week has no wanted carousel, and no carousel job was asked for that week. |
| `POST /review/jobs` `{kind:"topic", site, text, networks?, startDate?, time?, brand?}` | one post written from **the customer's own words** about the week (an offer, a new dish, an event). Her text (10 to 1000 characters) is kept as typed in `customer-facts.json` beside the brand and turned into facts whose source is `customer-statement:<id>`: it is never called verified, it is used only for its own post (a normal run never writes from it), and the claim gate holds the post to what she wrote (a figure, price or date she did not write is turned back). Refused with `400 INVALID` and a plain sentence: a link in the text (links are not read in this box), a claim that needs proof (a guarantee or superlative, a certificate or award, a medical or results claim, a customer count, a comparison), or too little or too much text. The website must already have been read (`400` otherwise). A product's customer (`brand` must be the one in `X-Brand`) is written for only her connected channels (`409 CONFLICT` "CONNECTED_UNKNOWN" until they are known), and may add one topic a week (Monday to Sunday, India time, from the day of the post): beyond that `429 {error, code:"TOPIC_LIMIT_REACHED", restart}`; a failed topic does not use the week. Staff may add as many as they like, for a brand named after its site. Answers `202 {job, created}` (`kind:"topic"`; a second ask while one runs is the same job and its words are not kept). The job writes ONE post as a batch of its own (`--copy-first --topic <id> --days 1`) for the owner to keep, edit or drop before any picture is drawn. The words never appear in the job. |
| `GET /review/carousels` | the carousels made for the product's brand (staff: `?brand=`), newest first: `{carousels:[{id, topic, date, createdAt, planFile, count, status}]}`. |
| `GET /review/carousels/<brand>/<id>`, `POST .../approve`, `POST .../reject` `{reason, comment?}` | one carousel whole, and approve or reject it as one step. A product may only reach its own brand's (`403`). The slides and captions of a carousel are not in `GET /review/posts` for a product, and `approve`, `reject`, `redraw` and `words` on one of them answer `409 CONFLICT` ("decide the carousel as a whole"). |
| `GET /review/posts?status=pending\|approved\|...` | the pictures: `{posts[{versionId, postId, planFile, network, shape, width, height, headline, caption, imageSha256, versionNumber, status, ...}], problems, reasons}` |
| `GET /review/posts/<versionId>`, `/image`, `/logo`, `/download` (approved only) | one picture with its history and evidence |
| `POST /review/posts/<versionId>/approve`, `/reject` `{reason, comment?}` | per picture; both give the same answer when repeated. A new version replaces only its own network's older one (`superseded`, `409 STALE` on approve). Reject also withdraws an approval; whether it has already been released is not checked yet (piece 8). |
| `POST /review/posts/<versionId>/words` `{caption}` | new words for a drawn picture: a new **pending** version of the same picture (same bytes, no draw, no cost) with the new caption, for that version's network only; the hashtags it carries stay, a typed closing run of #tags is not added twice. The older version becomes `superseded` (an approval of it stops being in force; the release pass drops that network until the new version is approved) and the other networks of the post are untouched. Answers `200 {version:{...summary, status:"pending"}}`. `422 REJECTED` for words that fail the checks; `400 INVALID` if `headline` is sent (it is printed on the picture, so a new headline needs a new picture); `409 CONFLICT` for the same words, `409 BLOCKED` for a blocked picture, `409 STALE` for a superseded one; `404` for another brand; `403` for staff on a product brand. |
| `POST /review/posts/<versionId>/redraw` | draw one more picture for that version's network only (the other networks of the post keep theirs). Allowed for the newest picture while it is pending, rejected, blocked or held, never while approved (withdraw first). One redraw per poster by default; a try that bought nothing does not use it. Answers `202 {job, created}`; a second click while it waits answers `200` with the same job; beyond the allowance `409 {code:"LIMIT"}`. The new picture arrives as a new pending version. Each poster in the list and in a picture's detail carries `canRedraw` and `redrawsLeft`. |
| *(draw, look-alike check)* | After a topic's pictures are drawn for two or more networks, they are compared side by side (one vision request per topic; `LOOKALIKE_CHECK=off` turns it off). Of a pair that is clearly the same scene, the later network is drawn once more with a note to choose a different scene; it counts as a picture of that channel (within the weekly 7); when the second try is not accepted or a limit stops it, the first picture stays. A comparison that cannot be made redraws nothing. Not done in placeholder mode. |
| `GET /review/plan` | where the customer's plan ends: `{brand, startsOn, endsOn}` (first and last post day of the batches not put away, drawn ones included; null when she has none). Staff: `?brand=`. |
| `GET /review/expiry?since=<iso>` | what was put away because nobody decided in time: `{expired[{at, brand, kind:"batch"\|"poster"\|"week", planFile, postId?, network?, versionId?}]}` for the caller's brand, oldest first. For product-owned brands only, 14 days after creation: an undrawn written batch is closed and a waiting picture is rejected with `reason:"expired"` by `system:expiry`. Decided posters (approved or rejected) are left alone. The service looks hourly; the customer notice is the product's to send. "expired" cannot be chosen by a caller. |
| `POST /review/topics/<brand>/<planFile>/<postId>/approve` | approve every network's waiting picture of one topic; a repeated call answers `200` with the same decisions and `already:true` |

## Sign-off on the picture

For every brand a poster carries the headline and the web address only: the brand's name is not drawn by default (the brand's real logo, when it has one, is a separate step and is unaffected). `signoffDefault` in the copy list says so for every brand: `{"brandName":"","domain":"<site address>"}`, so a screen shows an empty name box and the real address. A name typed for one post (`brandName` in the edit call, before drawing) is drawn on that post; an empty name is the default again, and clearing the address leaves that line off.

## Release

Approved pictures of a product-owned brand are released by the same pass as our own clients' (the campaign runner's release pass, hourly, only while publishing is switched on). Asking for a draw writes the release record under the customer's brand name (`client` in the body is ignored for products), so each approved network's picture and caption go out under that brand, to the networks they were written for. A tested sequence: nothing approved, nothing scheduled; approve the topic, both networks are scheduled; withdraw one picture, it leaves the schedule at the next pass and the other stays; withdraw both, nothing is scheduled.

The review service does not know when the publisher has already sent a post, so it cannot refuse a withdrawal after that point. For a post already in the publisher's schedule or sent, the product layer decides from the publisher's own state (skip the planned post).

## Not in v1 yet

Customer-safe wording for errors and for blocked or held posters (the service gives `status` blocked or held and `canRedraw`; the wording is the product's), and the manual-only approval lock (the approval-mode switch lives in the publisher and the vps session's work, not in this branch).
